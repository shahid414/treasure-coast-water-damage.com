Quick Answer

Treasure Coast Water Damage Restoration is a local water damage restoration team serving Stuart, Port St. Lucie and Fort Pierce — extraction, drying, cleanup and repairs under one written scope, with a free inspection on every job. Call (772) 555-0100 any time.

What We Do

Treasure Coast Water Damage Restoration helps homeowners and small businesses recover from water damage — from the first emergency call to the final repair. We extract standing water, dry structures to a verified standard, clean up flood and sewage contamination, remediate mold, and repair the drywall, flooring and cabinetry that water ruins. One team, one written scope, one point of contact for the whole job.

Our service area is the Treasure Coast we live and work in: all of Stuart (ZIP codes 34994, 34996 and 34997), plus nearby Port St. Lucie and Fort Pierce across Martin and St. Lucie counties. When you call our local (772) number, you reach people who know these neighborhoods — the older homes near downtown Stuart with aging plumbing, the slab-on-grade subdivisions where leaks hide under flooring, and the condos and rentals where a neighbor's leak becomes your ceiling stain.

How We Work

Free inspection, written quote

Every job starts with a free on-site inspection. We measure moisture with meters and thermal imaging, photograph the damage, and give you an itemized written quote before any work begins. No verbal-only numbers, no work you did not approve.

Verified drying, not guesswork

We dry to a standard we can prove: moisture readings in affected materials must match dry readings from unaffected areas of your home before equipment comes out. You see the readings at every visit.

Documentation for your claim

Photos, daily moisture logs and an itemized scope of everything removed and repaired. Whether your insurer pays in full, in part, or not at all, you will have a complete record of the loss and the work.

Honest about what can be saved

Some materials dry out fine; others — sewage-soaked pad, swollen particleboard, moldy drywall — have to come out. We will show you the readings and explain the reasoning either way, and we never sell you work your home does not need.

What You Can Expect From Us

  • A real person answers. Water damage does not keep business hours, and neither do we.
  • Clear arrival windows. When you call, we tell you when a crew can realistically be there — and we update you if that changes.
  • Daily updates during drying. You will know the moisture readings, what the equipment is doing, and when we expect to finish.
  • Respect for your home. Floor protection, contained work areas, and a cleaned-up site at the end of every visit.
  • Straight answers on cost. Typical ranges are published on our cost page, and your quote is itemized in writing before work starts.

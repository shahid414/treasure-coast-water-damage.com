Quick Answer

Short version: shut off the water, cut power to wet areas, photograph the damage, and call (772) 555-0100 — we answer. Drying typically takes 3 to 5 days to a meter-verified dry standard, and sudden water losses are often covered by homeowners insurance. Details below.

Emergencies & First Steps

Shut off the water source (main valve or appliance supply) if you can do so safely. Turn off power to affected areas if water is near outlets. Move valuables and fabrics to dry ground, take photos of the damage, and call us at (772) 555-0100. Do not walk through standing water if it might be electrically charged or sewage-contaminated, and stay out of rooms with sagging ceilings.

We answer and dispatch immediately. For active flooding in Stuart we target arrival within about an hour, depending on conditions and crew availability. We confirm a realistic arrival window on the call — no vague promises.

If the source is a pipe or appliance, yes — shut off the water to stop the damage from growing. If water is near outlets, appliances or your breaker panel, turn off power to those areas and stay out of the water until it is safe. We will talk you through it on the phone.

Cost & Insurance

Typical ranges: emergency water extraction $1,500–$4,500, structural drying $2,000–$6,000, flood cleanup $2,500–$9,000, and single-room repairs after drying $500–$2,500. The final price depends on water volume, rooms affected, materials and drying days. Your inspection is free and your quote is written before work begins. See our cost guide for the full breakdown.

Sudden, accidental water damage from inside the home — burst pipes, appliance failures — is often covered. Long-term leaks, maintenance issues and flooding from outside surface water usually are not, and mold and sewage coverage is often limited or requires an endorsement. We document moisture readings, photos and an itemized scope to support your claim, but coverage decisions belong to your insurer.

Yes. We inspect the damage, measure moisture, and give you a written, itemized quote at no cost and with no obligation. You decide whether to proceed.

Drying & Repairs

Most homes reach a verified dry standard in 3 to 5 days. Hardwood, tile assemblies and multi-layer flooring take longer; a single room with drywall and carpet can be faster. We check moisture daily and remove equipment only when readings match dry areas of your home.

Sometimes. Carpet can often be saved if clean water is extracted quickly with specialty tools; the pad underneath usually cannot. Hardwood may survive if dried promptly, while laminate and swollen particleboard typically cannot. Sewage-contaminated porous materials almost always must be removed. We will show you the moisture readings and explain what can realistically be saved.

Mitigation is the emergency half: stop the source, extract water, remove what cannot be saved, and dry the structure. Restoration is the rebuild that follows: new drywall, matched texture, flooring, cabinets and paint. We handle both under one written scope.

Often, yes — drying equipment is noisy and rooms with equipment are usually closed off, but many families stay in unaffected parts of the home. If sewage contamination or extensive mold is involved, we may recommend staying elsewhere until disinfection and removal are complete. We will advise you honestly at the inspection.

Mold & Health

In Florida conditions, mold can begin growing on wet drywall, wood and carpet backing within 24 to 48 hours. That is why extraction and drying in the first day matter so much — and why hidden moisture must be verified dry, not assumed dry.

A persistent musty smell after a leak usually means hidden moisture — often inside a wall cavity or under flooring — and possibly hidden mold. Book a free moisture inspection: meters and thermal imaging will show where the moisture is, and we will explain your options. See our mold remediation page.

Yes. Sewage water carries bacteria and viruses and is treated as the highest contamination category in restoration. Keep people and pets out of the area, stop using water if multiple drains are backing up, and call for professional sewage cleanup rather than attempting it yourself.

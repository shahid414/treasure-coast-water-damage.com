Water Damage Restoration in Stuart, FL — Done Right, Start to Finish

Water damage moves fast. A burst supply line can put hundreds of gallons into a Stuart home in under an hour, and in Florida's heat and humidity, wet drywall, flooring and cabinets start growing mold within 24 to 48 hours. The first hours matter more than anything else — which is why our crews answer around the clock and prioritize stopping the water, extracting what is standing, and getting professional drying equipment running the same visit.

Treasure Coast Water Damage Restoration handles the whole job under one roof: emergency water extraction, structural drying verified with moisture meters, cleanup of flood and sewage water, mold remediation, and the repairs that put your home back together. You get one accountable local team, one written scope, and documentation your insurance adjuster can actually use — photos, daily moisture readings and an itemized estimate.

We serve all of Stuart (ZIP codes 34994, 34996 and 34997) and nearby Port St. Lucie and Fort Pierce. Whether it is a ruptured water heater in the garage, an overflowing upstairs tub, a washing-machine hose that let go, or storm water pushed in during a Treasure Coast downpour, the process is the same: stop the source, pull the water out, dry the structure to a verified dry standard, then repair what could not be saved.

Common Questions

We answer 24/7 and dispatch immediately. For active flooding in Stuart we target arrival within about an hour, depending on conditions and crew availability. When you call, we confirm a realistic arrival window on the spot.

Sudden, accidental water damage — like a burst pipe or appliance failure — is often covered, while long-term leaks and flood water from outside usually are not. We provide photos, moisture logs and an itemized scope to support your claim, but coverage decisions are made by your insurer.

Shut off the water at the main valve if you can do so safely, turn off electricity to affected areas if water is near outlets, move valuables to dry ground, and call us. Avoid walking through standing water if there is any chance it is electrically charged or contaminated with sewage.

Most homes reach a verified dry standard in 3 to 5 days, depending on materials and how long they stayed wet. We check moisture daily with meters and remove equipment only when readings confirm the structure is dry.

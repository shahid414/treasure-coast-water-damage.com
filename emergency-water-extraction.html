Why Extraction Speed Matters

Water does not sit still. Within minutes it wicks into drywall, baseboards, cabinet toe-kicks and the padding under carpet. Within hours, hardwood and laminate flooring begin to cup and swell, and particleboard cabinets start to come apart. In Stuart's warm, humid climate, mold can begin growing on wet materials within 24 to 48 hours. Every hour standing water stays in your home, more of it becomes unsalvageable — and the repair bill grows with it.

Professional extraction is not the same as a wet vacuum from the hardware store. Truck-mounted and high-capacity portable extractors pull water out of carpet and padding far more thoroughly, and weighted extraction tools can sometimes save carpet that would otherwise be thrown away. Removing the bulk water first is also what makes the drying phase faster and cheaper: dehumidifiers cannot keep up with a floor that is still holding gallons of water.

Common Causes We Respond To in Stuart

  • Burst or leaking supply lines — under sinks, behind refrigerators and washing machines, and inside walls in older Stuart homes.
  • Water heater failures — a ruptured tank can release 40 to 80 gallons into a garage or utility closet in minutes.
  • Appliance overflows — washing machines, dishwashers and upstairs tubs or toilets that overflow into ceilings below.
  • AC condensate backups — clogged drain lines are a frequent, sneaky source of ceiling and closet water damage in Florida.
  • Storm and flood water — wind-driven rain and street flooding during Treasure Coast storms and hurricanes.

What Happens When You Call

  1. We help you stop the source. On the phone we walk you through shutting off the main water valve or the appliance supply, and turning off power to affected areas if water is near outlets.
  2. A crew is dispatched immediately. We confirm a realistic arrival window on the call — for active flooding in Stuart we target about an hour.
  3. Extraction begins on arrival. Standing water is pumped and extracted from every affected room, including water trapped under flooring where accessible.
  4. We document everything. Photos, moisture readings and an itemized scope of what was wet and what was removed — the paperwork your insurance adjuster needs.
  5. Drying starts the same visit. Air movers and dehumidifiers are set up before we leave, so the structure starts drying immediately. See structural drying.

What To Do While You Wait

  • Shut off the main water valve if the source is a pipe or appliance.
  • Turn off electricity to wet areas at the breaker if water is near outlets or appliances — and stay out of standing water until you are sure it is safe.
  • Move furniture, rugs and valuables to dry ground; lift curtains and fabrics off wet floors.
  • Take photos of the damage before anything is moved — helpful for insurance.
  • Do not use a household vacuum on water, and do not enter rooms with sagging ceilings.

When the water is out, the job is not finished — hidden moisture in walls and subfloors still needs professional drying. That is why extraction and structural drying are almost always scoped together.

Emergency Mitigation: Stop the Damage First

The first phase of any water loss is mitigation — stopping the source, removing standing water, tearing out materials that cannot be saved, and drying the structure before mold takes hold. In Stuart's humidity, that window is short: mold can start growing on wet drywall within 24 to 48 hours. These services are available around the clock.

Emergency Water Extraction

Rapid removal of standing water from floors, carpets and crawl spaces with truck-mounted and portable extractors. The faster water comes out, the more of your home we can save.

Typical range: $1,500 – $4,500

Cleanup & Health-Safety Services

Not all water is equal. Sewage backups and long-standing moisture create health hazards that need specialized handling, protective equipment and proper disposal — not a shop vacuum and a fan.

Sewage Cleanup

Sewage-contaminated water carries bacteria and viruses. We extract and dispose of it safely, disinfect affected surfaces, and dry the area so it is safe to occupy again.

Mold Remediation

Storm Damage Restoration

Treasure Coast storms push water in through roofs, windows and doors, and can leave a home wet from several directions at once. Our storm damage restoration service covers emergency extraction and drying after wind-driven rain and flooding, plus the repairs — drywall, flooring, cabinets and paint — documented step by step for your insurance claim.

Repairs After Drying

Once your home is verified dry, the rebuild begins: drywall replacement and texture matching, flooring (LVP, laminate, tile, hardwood), baseboards and trim, swollen cabinet boxes, and paint. A single contained room typically runs $500 – $2,500 after drying; multi-room losses with flooring and cabinetry run higher. Every repair is priced in a written, itemized scope you approve before work starts. See our cost guide for the full breakdown.
